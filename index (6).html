About our business

Cirebon has been the heart of Indonesian rattan craft for generations. Mr. Ridwan started this business to bring that craft to buyers overseas, with fair prices, honest communication and products that last.

We work with a network of local weavers and a small finishing workshop. Every item is sorted, dried, treated against insects and checked by hand before it is packed for export.

  • Natural, sustainably harvested rattan
  • Custom sizes, colors and weave patterns
  • Full export documents and safe packing
